# Comic Supervision Knowledge

Version 0.3.2 adds experimental guidance for the following 15 themes to the existing comedy supervision. Select the knowledge needed for a scene's intended effect, then develop expressive options for written scripts and thumbnails and improvements for PNG previews.

- [Comedy know-how](gag/README.md): mechanisms of humor, expectations and evidence, idea generation, and review.
- [Straight-man responses and reactions to the unusual](tsukkomi/README.md): correction, silence, letting things pass, character voice, and relationship effects. The stable ID `tsukkomi` names the Japanese comedy response role.
- [Non-explicit allure](allure/README.md): the observer, gestures, distance, departures from ordinary behavior, and moments of becoming aware of another person.
- [Coolness and stature](stature/README.md): evidence of skill, judgment, resolve, others' reactions, and coexistence with weakness.
- [Unease and fear](horror/README.md): departures from daily life, information presentation, changes while waiting, light and sound, and behavior.
- [Anger, arguments, and rupture](conflict/README.md): the dispute, disagreement, expressions of anger, crossed boundaries, and the subsequent relationship.
- [Grief, loss, and aftermath](grief/README.md): absence, changing habits, mixed emotions, timing of reactions, and what remains.
- [Affection, embarrassment, and budding romance](romance/README.md): who feels affection, awareness, special choices, and responses.
- [Intimacy and trust](trust/README.md): what is entrusted, roles, habits, and responses that preserve differences.
- [Awkwardness and misunderstanding](awkwardness/README.md): facts and interpretations, reasons not to ask, silence, and emotions after correction.
- [Crisis, anxiety, and urgency](urgency/README.md): what can be lost, remaining opportunities, attempts and outcomes, and waiting.
- [Surprise, discovery, and revelation](revelation/README.md): revised interpretations, clues, reveal placement, and reactions.
- [Exhilaration, reversal, and release](payoff/README.md): constraints, reasons circumstances change, rewards, and costs.
- [Endearing qualities and attachment](endearment/README.md): particular interests, different reactions, recovery, and coexistence with competence and responsibility.
- [Bargaining and probing](bargaining/README.md): each character's goals and information, responses to a move, and changing conditions.
- [Reconciliation, forgiveness, and solace](reconciliation/README.md): apology, reception, chosen distance, and resumed actions.
- [When and how to use supervision](routing.md): selecting necessary perspectives and the main effect.
- [Sources and scope](sources.md): primary sources for the 15 added themes, adaptation scope, and unverified matters.
- [Common knowledge](../review-workflow.md#common-knowledge): character, causality, information, reading order, and endings.

Procedures are consolidated in `manga-supervision`; the main agent integrates production decisions and acceptance. Use `skills/manga-supervision/SKILL.md` in the kit and `.agents/skills/manga-supervision/SKILL.md` in a project.

Regardless of the work's genre, use only themes needed by the target scene. Handle effects without dedicated guidance through existing production knowledge and review; do not describe them as supported by dedicated supervision.

The 15 added themes are experimental, based on source research and preliminary text review. General reader effects remain unverified and must be checked with each project's PNGs and trial reads. Distinguish inspecting illustrations in published sources, self-review of original exercises, and actual reader responses. The distributed supervision files are listed in `distribution.json`.
