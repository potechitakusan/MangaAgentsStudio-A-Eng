# Supervision: Awkwardness and Misunderstanding

Status: Experimental. Updated / sources checked: 2026-09-19. Covers gaps between intended and received meaning and scenes that continue with something unsaid. These are **production proposals** informed by [S18](../sources.md#s18) and [S23](../sources.md#s23).

For deliberate attempts to influence someone, see [Bargaining](../bargaining/README.md); for disputes and rejection, [Anger](../conflict/README.md). For knowledge differences and viewpoint, see [Character](../../manga/05-story-character.md); for pauses, [Pacing](../../manga/03-beat-pacing-timing.md).

<a id="w01"></a>

## W01 Separate disagreements in facts, knowledge, and interpretation

- Examine: Actual events, each person's knowledge, what they assume, and the reply they expected.
- Use when: A supposed misunderstanding has no traceable point of divergence.
- Compare: Show information only one person received, supply another meaning of the same words, or contrast an expected action with the actual one.
- Costs and exceptions: Explaining every thought reduces uncertainty. Do not relabel confirmed harm or lies as mutual misunderstanding.
- Basis: Organizing S18's mismatch between expression and inner life through common knowledge of information differences.

<a id="w02"></a>

## W02 Establish why they cannot ask immediately

- Examine: Concrete reasons to hesitate, such as onlookers, time, status, or a past reply.
- Use when: One question would solve matters, but nobody asks solely for plot convenience.
- Compare: Start to ask and rephrase, prioritize another task, or show the burden of asking itself.
- Costs and exceptions: Do not prolong matters by endlessly adding obstacles. You may choose either resolution through asking or persistent differences in values even after asking.
- Basis: S18 and character goals and constraints.

<a id="w03"></a>

## W03 Choose actions that change within silence

- Examine: Differences before and after silence while waiting for a reply, continuing cleanup, or returning to a topic.
- Use when: A series of silent faces makes comfort, boredom, and awkwardness hard to distinguish.
- Compare: Stop an unfinished action, omit the usual response, or change the target of a gaze while keeping distance constant.
- Costs and exceptions: Do not add sweat or black backgrounds to every silence. Overemphasizing a prop can create an unintended mystery.
- Basis: Applying S23's gestures and information density to differences within silence.

<a id="w04"></a>

## W04 Choose what only readers know

- Examine: Whose intention do readers know? Is the aim comic misunderstanding, painful silence, or uncertainty?
- Use when: A character's mistake is confused with readers lacking information.
- Compare: Show both sides, preserve one viewpoint, or provide only a clue that allows correction.
- Costs and exceptions: Revealing everything reduces mystery. Even when concealing information, make it possible to follow who reacted to what.
- Basis: S18's concealment of inner life and common knowledge.

<a id="w05"></a>

## W05 Do not erase feelings when the misunderstanding clears

- Examine: Problems solved by corrected information versus hurt feelings and different desires.
- Use when: Everyone smiles immediately after an explanation, skipping the relationship change.
- Compare: Add a brief apology, proceed only with the task, leave something still unsaid, or restart at a different distance.
- Costs and exceptions: A small misunderstanding can end immediately in laughter. Neither greater seriousness nor complete resolution is mandatory.
- Basis: S18 and earlier anger and relationship supervision.
