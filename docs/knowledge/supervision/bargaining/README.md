# Supervision: Bargaining and Probing

Status: Experimental. Updated / sources checked: 2026-09-19. Covers **fictional scenes** in which characters read another's responses and choose words or actions. Production proposals informed by [S22](../sources.md#s22) and [S17](../sources.md#s17), not methods for manipulating real people or guaranteed ways to win games.

For unintended differences in understanding, see [Misunderstanding](../awkwardness/README.md); for reveal order, [Revelation](../revelation/README.md). For true motives and knowledge differences, return to [Character](../../manga/05-story-character.md); for dialogue purpose, [Scenes](../../manga/04-scene-dialogue-props.md).

<a id="d01"></a>

## D01 Separate each person's desired gain from the stated goal

- Examine: What do they want to confirm, protect, or have the other do? Does the public proposal match the private desire?
- Use when: Implication-rich dialogue does not make success or failure meaningful.
- Compare: Reveal the motive only to readers, make the goal public while hiding means, or seek only one response.
- Costs and exceptions: Not everyone needs a secret. Even with visible interests, negotiation can concern room for concessions or timing of choices.
- Basis: S22's character goals and S17's organization of information.

<a id="d02"></a>

## D02 Distinguish knowledge, inference, and bluffing

- Examine: Where did the person obtain the information? Is a confident claim factual, or intended to make the other believe it?
- Use when: Characters anticipate events using information only the author knows.
- Compare: Supply an observed clue, gamble on an inference, or recover from a mistaken reading.
- Costs and exceptions: Do not turn a bluff into established fact. Observing a gesture still leaves its meaning an inference; without a special story premise, it does not confirm inner thoughts. Even if the basis is revealed later, preserve a plausible opportunity to obtain it.
- Basis: S17, S22, and common knowledge of information differences.

<a id="d03"></a>

## D03 Follow the response to each move

- Examine: What does the other receive from a question or proposal, how do they judge it, and how do they answer?
- Use when: “Everything went according to plan” alone is expected to establish a battle of wits.
- Compare: Elicit a concession, let the other add a condition, or have them notice the probe and change their response.
- Costs and exceptions: Explaining everything reduces tension. Focus on enough evidence to follow the crucial move.
- Basis: Organizing S22's choices and key points into an action/reaction chain.

<a id="d04"></a>

## D04 Leave room for a move not to work

- Examine: Could the other refuse, misunderstand, or act for a different goal, and with what consequences?
- Use when: Everyone follows a plan merely to make one person look clever.
- Compare: Use a second plan, lower conditions, withdraw, or accept unexpected cooperation.
- Costs and exceptions: A showcase with predetermined success is possible, but should not promise the same tension as a contest with an uncertain outcome.
- Basis: S22's approach to characters acting from their own thinking.

<a id="d05"></a>

## D05 Show conditions changed by the exchange

- Examine: What was gained or revealed, and which promises or distrust remain?
- Use when: A surprising explanation leaves the outcome of negotiation or the relationship unclear.
- Compare: Briefly state the scope of agreement, lose one secret, or leave a reason the other will not cooperate next time.
- Costs and exceptions: Do not automatically add praise for the deceiver. Explanation to readers and explanation to the other character can be chosen separately.
- Basis: S17's trust and S22's key points.

## Comparison, images, and trial reads

Keep each person's knowledge, goals, authority, and outcomes constant. Compare the basis for a move and how the reply is shown. In PNGs, check who is positioned to observe which reaction. In trial reads, ask who sought and gained what, and what supported their inferences. Comparative verification with actual project PNGs and readers has not been performed.
