# Supervision: Endearing Qualities and Attachment

Status: Experimental. Updated / sources checked: 2026-09-19. Covers gestures and reactions that make readers want to care about a person. These are **production proposals** informed by [S04](../sources.md#s04) and [S23](../sources.md#s23). Do not equate a cute appearance, affection from another character, and reader attachment.

For romantic feelings, see [Affection](../romance/README.md); for appealing skill or resolve, [Coolness](../stature/README.md). For voice, desires, and roles, return to [Character](../../manga/05-story-character.md); for props, [Scenes](../../manga/04-scene-dialogue-props.md).

<a id="e01"></a>

## E01 Specify what the person cares about

- Examine: What favorite food, routine, particular interest, or modest goal generates behavior?
- Use when: Visual decoration alone is being used to increase attachment.
- Compare: Show a little preparation, choose a carefully handled object, or show delight in reaching a goal.
- Costs and exceptions: A cute hobby need not be invented afterward. Choose from what the established character values.
- Basis: Combining S04's individual reactions with common knowledge of desire.

<a id="e02"></a>

## E02 Choose a contrast with normal behavior or a familiar good quality

- Examine: Does a composed person's fluster or a stern person's small delight connect to established personality?
- Use when: Someone becomes uncaring or incompetent immediately beforehand merely to create contrast.
- Compare: Show one difference from normal, repeat familiar conscientiousness, or show different responses to different people.
- Costs and exceptions: Contrast is optional. Consistently gentle people and scenes enjoyed for appearance alone can also work.
- Basis: Using S04's varied reactions within the constraints of an established character.

<a id="e03"></a>

## E03 Separate failure from the response after failure

- Examine: Is there a visible choice to cover up, retry, or ask for help?
- Use when: Only failure and hardship are increased to make someone endearing.
- Compare: Recover in a small way, ask bashfully, earnestly continue, or succeed and openly celebrate.
- Costs and exceptions: Do not lower skill or erase responsibility for real harm to others. Do not treat enjoying another's distress as a universal rule.
- Basis: S04 and character choice. The post-failure check is this kit's proposal.

<a id="e04"></a>

## E04 Allocate attention between gestures and others' judgments

- Examine: Is the person's own action readable? Are surrounding remarks of “cute” doing all the work?
- Use when: More praise and close-ups fail to convey individuality.
- Compare: Show ingenuity in the hands, notice a small reaction, or include just one observer.
- Costs and exceptions: Do not automatically infantilize proportions or speech or make everyone affectionate. Direct gaze at the reader and large facial views are options when suited to the scene.
- Basis: S23's focal points and gestures, and S04.

<a id="e05"></a>

## E05 Preserve attachment alongside judgment of the person

- Examine: After showing vulnerability or an endearing trait, do ability, conflict, and responsibility remain?
- Use when: Adding an enemy's human side is treated as immediate forgiveness by others.
- Compare: Care while stopping them, show just one appealing side, or preserve criticism of their behavior.
- Costs and exceptions: Not every character must be liked. Do not assert reader preferences before actually checking them.
- Basis: S04 and earlier stature and anger supervision.

## Comparison, images, and trial reads

Keep personality, skills, and responsibility constant. Compare failure / recovery or contrasting reactions / repetition. In PNGs, check gesture visibility and the target of the response. In trial reads, ask which act felt characteristic and whether anything made readers care about the person. Comparative verification with actual project PNGs and readers has not been performed.
