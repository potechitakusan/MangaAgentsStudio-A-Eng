# Comedy Supplement: Aligning Expectations and Evidence

Compiled: 2026-09-17. Seven supplements for aligning expectations and evidence. Use with G01–G23 and select production proposals suited to the characters and situation. These are not panel-count or layout rules; verify reader effects per project.

## GX01 Resolve a word's interpretation through an object's form

- Structure: The answer expected from a word or shape differs from the answer revealed by actually manipulating it.
- Production proposal: Establish a premise allowing two readings, then settle one through a visible result. Supplements G03 and G13.
- Script/PNG check: Examine changes in word meaning, letter shape, and operation order separately. Invent wording suited to these characters and this task.

## GX02 Separate an accurate explanation from the answer requested

- Structure: A reply can use correct knowledge and terminology yet fail to address the questioner's practical need.
- Production proposal: Separate what the expert tries to correct from what the other person wants to do. Supplements G18 and G19.
- Script/PNG check: Has the expert abruptly become incompetent? Is the mismatch in purpose readable without specialist knowledge?

## GX03 Let added convenience defeat the original purpose

- Structure: A mechanism or improvement that solves a difficulty works against the original purpose, such as hiding or retrieving something.
- Production proposal: Preserve the useful property and make its application conflict with the purpose. Supplements G08, G16, and G22.
- Script/PNG check: Is the inconvenience visibly caused by the attempted solution? Does the ending merely add an unrelated accident?

## GX04 Reinterpret an impressive appearance through everyday circumstances

- Structure: A seemingly grave, grand, or philosophically detached state turns out to have a mundane or bodily cause.
- Production proposal: Preserve the impressive-looking state while shifting the evaluative scale or cause to something small. Supplements G12 and G20.
- Script/PNG check: Is there evidence both for the original impression and for the true circumstances? Is serious harm also being trivialized?

## GX05 Make a helpful strength conflict with a personal goal

- Structure: Giving another person correct advice works against the adviser's competitive wishes.
- Production proposal: Preserve kindness and expertise while using another role the character simultaneously occupies. Supplements G08, G18, and G21.
- Script/PNG check: Does helping lead to the result, and is the reason it troubles the helper visible? Preserve the fact that the act was well-intentioned.

## GX06 Reveal a different property of the same object at the end

- Structure: A later visible mark or result revises the expected property or quantity of the same object.
- Production proposal: Do not swap the object itself; reveal a change in one condition determining its value. Supplements G13, G14, and G23.
- Script/PNG check: Is the evidence meaningful to readers? Is the drop in value readable without canceling the success of obtaining the object?

## GX07 Separate the predictor's goal from the actor's goal

- Structure: One person predicts another's behavior in detail, but the other acts according to a different task or standard.
- Production proposal: Ground the failed prediction in the other person's independent desire. Supplements G02, G13, and G17.
- Script/PNG check: Can readers follow the prediction? After it fails, does the actual behavior read as coherent?
