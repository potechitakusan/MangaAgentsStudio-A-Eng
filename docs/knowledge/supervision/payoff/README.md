# Supervision: Exhilaration, Reversal, and Release

Status: Experimental. Updated / sources checked: 2026-09-19. Covers showcases where circumstances change after accumulated constraints or expectations. These are **production proposals** informed by [S21](../sources.md#s21) and [S22](../sources.md#s22). Do not measure exhilaration by the quantity of victory or revenge.

For establishing constraints, see [Urgency](../urgency/README.md); for reinterpretation of information, [Revelation](../revelation/README.md); for impressions of a powerful figure, [Stature](../stature/README.md). For shared resolution and aftermath guidance, return to [Story](../../manga/05-story-character.md).

<a id="p01"></a>

## P01 Decide what the release is from

- Examine: Is the change in winning or losing, restricted action, unspoken feelings, or a long-awaited task?
- Use when: Spectacular success is weakly connected to earlier difficulty.
- Compare: Make one previously impossible action possible, obtain the desired reply, or secure only what needs protection.
- Costs and exceptions: Defeat or withdrawal can also bring release. Not every difficulty must be resolved.
- Basis: S21's changes of meaning through events and common knowledge.

<a id="p02"></a>

## P02 Ground the reversal in established conditions

- Examine: Do ingenuity, choices, or cooperation connect to the result? Have an opponent's abilities or rules suddenly changed?
- Use when: An unknown power or convenient rescue appears only at the reversal.
- Compare: Reuse an established property, choose a reserved option, or let another person respond for their own reasons.
- Costs and exceptions: Coincidental help can remain when intended, but distinguish it from the character's own achievement.
- Basis: Applying S22's key points and choices to reversal causality.

<a id="p03"></a>

## P03 Give recurring words or actions changed meaning

- Examine: Does this action change how an earlier failure, habitual phrase, or promise is received?
- Use when: A forceful signature line lacks connection to the scene's events.
- Compare: Return the same words to someone else, finish the same task through a different choice, or fulfill an old promise without dialogue.
- Costs and exceptions: More flashbacks or repetition reduces momentum. New dialogue can also work; repetition is optional.
- Basis: S21's repeated language and changing meaning.

<a id="p04"></a>

## P04 Allocate the turning moment and time to absorb its result

- Examine: Can readers follow the decisive act, evidence of success or failure, and surrounding responses in order?
- Use when: Large panels or screams do not clarify what changed.
- Compare: Enlarge the decisive move, show the result first, or include a small moment of relief.
- Costs and exceptions: More universal praise is unnecessary. Quiet achievement or direct transition to another crisis are also options.
- Basis: S22 and common action/reaction order.

<a id="p05"></a>

## P05 Preserve gains and costs separately

- Examine: Which problems did the outcome solve, and what damage, loss, or responsibility remains?
- Use when: Earlier harm or others' choices disappear to create an exhilarating ending.
- Compare: Celebrate victory while showing the next responsibility, finish the task and rest, or confirm just one person's relief.
- Costs and exceptions: Every cost need not be explained for the aftermath. Do not automatically intensify a loser's humiliation or cruelty.
- Basis: S21, earlier grief and stature supervision, and common knowledge.

## Comparison, images, and trial reads

Keep abilities, resources, outcome, and costs constant. Compare use of a signature line and allocation between action and reaction. In PNGs, check before/after states and evidence of resolution. In trial reads, ask what was rewarded and why circumstances changed. Comparative verification with actual project PNGs and readers has not been performed. Follow [Common procedures](../routing.md).
