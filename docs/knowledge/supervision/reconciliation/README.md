# Supervision: Reconciliation, Forgiveness, and Solace

Status: Experimental. Updated / sources checked: 2026-09-19. Covers the relationships and ways of living characters choose after conflict or hurt. These are **production proposals** informed by [S18](../sources.md#s18), [S17](../sources.md#s17), and earlier [Anger](../conflict/README.md), [Grief](../grief/README.md), and [Trust](../trust/README.md) guidance. The distinctions below are this kit's organization, not a reconciliation process proposed by the cited creators.

For conflicting desires, choices, and scene endings, return to [Story and character](../../manga/05-story-character.md). Do not require every relationship to end in making up.

<a id="q01"></a>

## Q01 Distinguish how much changes this time

- Examine: Does the scene stop a fight, establish the circumstances, receive an apology, forgive, resume contact, or continue life?
- Use when: One apology or rescue is treated as achieving everything at once.
- Compare: Cooperate only on a task, hear the apology without restoring the relationship, or keep distance and begin a new life.
- Costs and exceptions: Full reconciliation is also possible. Support the intended change through responses in the scene. Solace is possible when the other person is absent or dead; face-to-face apology or resumed contact is not required.
- Basis: This kit's organization informed by S18's gap between inner life and external understanding, and the earlier themes.

<a id="q02"></a>

## Q02 Separate understanding circumstances from responsibility for behavior

- Examine: What does the person admit doing, and which effects do they face? Does explaining a motive erase the act?
- Use when: Revealing history or vulnerability makes the other's hurt or refusal disappear.
- Compare: Acknowledge the specific act, address actual harm, or listen to how the other received it.
- Costs and exceptions: An exhaustive courtroom-style explanation is unnecessary. Do not mechanically split responsibility for one-sided harm.
- Basis: S18, disputes underlying anger, and common character knowledge.

<a id="q03"></a>

## Q03 Connect choices after an apology to feasible action

- Examine: What changes next and what continues? Is trust treated as restored by words alone?
- Use when: An apology exists but no visible change supports the subsequent relationship.
- Compare: Make and keep a smaller promise, return what can be returned, or maintain the distance the other wants.
- Costs and exceptions: The magnitude of restitution does not buy a right to forgiveness. If immediate action is impossible, show the constraint and chosen course.
- Basis: Applying S17's scope of action and trust to rebuilding relationships.

<a id="q04"></a>

## Q04 Give the recipient time to choose and reply

- Examine: What can the other accept, and what can they not yet accept? Is forgiveness being inferred from a facial expression?
- Use when: Only the apologizer receives a showcase, while the recipient responds merely to deliver the ending.
- Compare: Set conditions, defer a decision, refuse, or reach out voluntarily.
- Costs and exceptions: Silent acceptance can work, but check how much surrounding actions establish. Do not treat a delayed response as immaturity.
- Basis: S18's distinction between expression and inner life.

<a id="q05"></a>

## Q05 Verify solace through actions the person can resume

- Examine: What can the character choose for themselves at the end: today's task, asking someone for advice, or a new relationship?
- Use when: A bright line or embrace alone is added to create an impression of resolution.
- Compare: Resume one ordinary activity, share the same place at a different distance, or move on without meeting the other person.
- Costs and exceptions: Unresolved feelings may remain. Undoing loss or rupture is not the only form of solace.
- Basis: S18 and common knowledge of grief and endings, not guidance for psychological recovery.

In trial reads, ask what was resolved, what remained, and what each person chose. Comparative verification with actual project PNGs and readers has not been performed.
