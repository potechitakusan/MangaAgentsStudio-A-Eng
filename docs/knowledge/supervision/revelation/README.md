# Supervision: Surprise, Discovery, and Revelation

Status: Experimental. Updated / sources checked: 2026-09-19. Covers new information changing the interpretation of events or characters. These are **production proposals** informed by [S20](../sources.md#s20) and [S23](../sources.md#s23). Quantity of foreshadowing does not guarantee surprise.

For shifts in advantage, see [Payoff](../payoff/README.md); for tension about what happens next, [Urgency](../urgency/README.md). For viewpoint and premises, return to [Character and information](../../manga/05-story-character.md); for presentation order, [Pacing](../../manga/03-beat-pacing-timing.md).

<a id="v01"></a>

## V01 Decide which interpretation changes after the reveal

- Examine: What situation can readers infer before and after the new information? Is the surprised party a reader or a character?
- Use when: Large reactions exist, but what is unexpected remains unclear.
- Compare: Change the reason for an earlier action, reveal an object's use, or let only the character's understanding catch up.
- Costs and exceptions: A character's discovery works even when readers know first. Reader predictions need not be overturned every time.
- Basis: Organizing S20's setup through common knowledge of information differences.

<a id="v02"></a>

## V02 Compare supplied clues with the later explanation

- Examine: Do earlier images, dialogue, and object placement remain valid after the reveal? Is information a character knows being unnaturally concealed?
- Use when: Newly introduced settings alone reinterpret everything that happened.
- Compare: Plant a clue with a natural initial function, reinterpret an existing object, or narrow the later explanation.
- Costs and exceptions: Not every surprise requires clues from which it could be deduced. When aiming for satisfying mystery resolution, check for consistency on rereading.
- Basis: A review method informed by S20. Match standards of fairness to the work's intent.

<a id="v03"></a>

## V03 Check where the answer first becomes visible

- Examine: Does dialogue, background, a spread, or a thumbnail expose the answer earlier than intended?
- Use when: A reveal relies on a page turn or information outside the current view.
- Compare: Refine the preceding question, adjust how much of the answer is shown, or structure early visibility as anticipation.
- Costs and exceptions: Do not hide necessary action just to conceal the answer. Simultaneous display may not reproduce a print page turn.
- Basis: Reviewing S23's page turns for the publication format.

<a id="v04"></a>

## V04 Allocate the moment of understanding and the reaction

- Examine: What makes the information meaningful, and what action follows? Are explanation and a surprised face repeating the same information?
- Use when: Lengthy explanation loses momentum, or excessive omission prevents understanding.
- Compare: Show one piece of evidence and move to the reaction, insert a short confirmation, or demonstrate understanding through changed behavior.
- Costs and exceptions: Silence is not always superior. Keep explanation when the reasoning process is part of the enjoyment.
- Basis: S23 and common reaction-order knowledge.

<a id="v05"></a>

## V05 Examine what surprise leaves for the next choice or reread

- Examine: Does the revelation change behavior, relationships, or goals? Is it consistent with existing characterization?
- Use when: Adding an unexpected fact becomes an end in itself.
- Compare: Reinterpret a past choice, change present means, or show that the choice remains the same despite new knowledge.
- Costs and exceptions: A small joke aimed only at momentary surprise can work. Prediction by a reader alone does not establish failure.
- Basis: S20 and common knowledge of character and endings.

## Comparison, images, and trial reads

Keep the truth, established facts, and character knowledge constant. Compare clues and reveal placement. In PNGs, check answer visibility in the intended viewing format. In trial reads, do not reveal the answer first; ask what readers thought before the reveal and where that changed. Comparative verification with actual project PNGs and readers has not been performed. Follow [Common procedures](../routing.md).
