# Scene Supervision: Sources and Scope

Source identity records below retain their original Japanese wording, including article titles, credited names, publishers, and historical dates. Claims, applications, and limitations are explained in English.

Retrieved / text checked: 2026-09-19. S01–S08 document sources used when adding straight-man responses and non-explicit allure; S09–S15 cover coolness, fear, anger, and grief; S16–S23 cover affection, trust, misunderstanding, urgency, revelation, reversal, endearment, bargaining, and reconciliation. This register does not retroactively substantiate the provenance of the earlier comedy documents.

These are creators' and editors' methods and views, not experimentally demonstrated laws of reader response. “Production proposals” are this kit's judgments informed by the sources and existing common knowledge. Distinguish source statements, observations of illustrations, and hypotheses about adaptation. Treat an original and its republication as the same testimony.

## S01 Character position and the straight-man response

- Source (original Japanese record): [ジャンプの漫画学校講義録⑦](https://jump-manga-school.hatenablog.com/entry/07)。講師：服部雄二郎。公開：2021-03-24、講義：2020-10-31。
- Confirmed claim: Discusses protagonist likability and relationships with other characters, including advantages of a protagonist who serves as the straight-man responder.
- Application: T01 and T05, considering the responder's position and impressions of the character.
- Limits: An editor's views using Jump works. Neither a straight-man protagonist nor making every character likable is mandatory. Unpublished lecture material mentioned at the end of the article was not checked.

## S02 Dialogue selection and whether to include a straight-man response

- Source (original Japanese record): [増田こうすけ先生×左藤真大先生・漫画執筆について](https://jumpsq.shueisha.co.jp/contents/manganogokui1/interview2.html)。発信：集英社ジャンプSQ.。公開日：本文では確認できず。
- Confirmed claim: Masuda describes limiting dialogue and choosing a form with comic instigator and responder after a run of works without a straight-man response.
- Application: T02 and T03, comparing explanation quantity and expressive form.
- Limits: Not evidence that short dialogue or omitting a response is always better. The featured collected volumes were not read in full.

## S04 Reactions and differences from ordinary behavior

- Source (original Japanese record): [赤坂アカ先生のキャラクター講座](https://www.clipstudio.net/oekaki/archives/153296)。発信：セルシス、集英社シンマン賞企画の転載。公開日：本文では確認できず。
- Confirmed claim: A method of constructing a character's ordinary personality and surrounding cast from the reaction the creator wants to depict. The main subject is cuteness and approachability.
- Application: T04, A02, A04, L03, and E01–E05, considering contrasts between ordinary and changed states. The text was checked again when endearment guidance was added.
- Limits: Applying a method for cuteness to allure is this kit's hypothesis. Do not make coldness, putting characters under pressure, or similar choices universal requirements.

## S05 Allure in clothed figures

## S07 Turning emotion into character action

## S09 Kinds of strength, opponents' ability, and individual expressions

- Source (original Japanese record): [真島ヒロ先生のお悩み教室](https://debut.shonenmagazine.com/interview/1021/)。発信：講談社「マガジンで描く！」。記事は2016年当時の企画と明記。ウェブ公開日は本文で確認できず。
- Confirmed claim: Discusses strength beyond physical power as part of coolness, avoiding overly weak-looking opponents, varying the scale of showcases, and changing emotional expression by character and scene.
- Application: K01–K04 and R03. The kit organizes several original answers into scene-level decision items.
- Limits: The author's method, not evidence that strength is the only appeal. Do not require spreads or specific panel counts in every publication format.

## S10 Everyday life, supporting characters, and conflicting beliefs

- Source (original Japanese record): [水上悟志先生インタビュー](https://note.com/tokyomangalab/n/n01d2beb23f0d)。発信：取材者の東京マンガラボ。掲載：2018-01-28。
- Confirmed claim: Emphasizes characters with everyday lives, treatment of supporting characters, and differing personal convictions, without directly equating victory and defeat with strength or righteousness.
- Application: K01–K03, K05, R02, and R05. Material for developing characters and avoiding oversimplified conflict.
- Limits: The author's views on shonen manga. Daily-life depiction, growth, and multiple righteous causes are not mandatory. Do not read this as equalizing responsibility for one-sided harm.

## S11 Black, white, and linework in frightening images

- Source (original Japanese record): [伊藤潤二─恐怖へと誘引する黒](https://iok.site/issue/i251031/)。発信：黒の研究所。公開日は取得した本文では確認できず。
- Confirmed claim: Describes black/white balance, areas to darken, and differences between solid blacks and crosshatching through the author's own experience and methods.
- Application: H01 and H04, considering departures from everyday normality and visual choices matching the focus.
- Limits: References to Kazuo Umezz and others reflect Ito's interpretation, not those creators' own testimony. This is not a quantitative law that more black means more fear. No independent visual inspection of illustrations was performed for this source.

- Source (original Japanese record): [「口に関するアンケート」背筋先生・押見修造先生インタビュー](https://shonenjumpplus.com/article/entry/kuchini_interview)。発信：集英社・少年ジャンプ＋。公開：2026-08-04。

- Limits: Creator intentions and the original author's impressions, not a comparative test with general readers. Do not treat wording about potential effects as proven results. The entire work was not checked.

## S13 The meaning of anger, character values, and voice

- Source (original Japanese record): [たりほ氏へのインタビュー・創作の背景](https://realsound.jp/book/2023/05/post-1316713_2.html)。発信：Real Sound、取材：望月悠木。公開：2023-05-01。

## S14 Life within loss and emotions not invented afterward

- Source (original Japanese record): [宮川サトシ氏が母との死別を漫画にした理由](https://kaigo.homes.co.jp/tayorini/interview/miyagawa/)。発信：tayorini by LIFULL介護。公開：2019-04-09、更新：2021-06-01。
- Confirmed claim: Describes working from notes of actual experience, not adding tears that did not occur, and depicting life including meals and laughter. Also describes changed perspectives over time.
- Application: G01–G05, considering personal responses, daily life, and differences by moment.
- Limits: One creator's experience and approach, not evidence of universal grief or recovery. Do not turn it into a rule against depicting tears. Retrieval of comic illustrations failed; they were not used for visual confirmation.

## S15 Time remaining in objects and a position from which to depict absence

- Source (original Japanese record): [今日マチ子氏「るすばん猫きなこ」インタビュー](https://book.asahi.com/article/16384114)。発信：朝日新聞・好書好日、取材：横井周子。公開：2026-03-10。
- Confirmed claim: Describes sensing time through everyday objects seen during field research and reconsidering her own assumption that elapsed time means an event is over.
- Application: G01, G02, G04, and G05, adapting clues to absence and distinguishing a scene's closure from emotional closure.
- Limits: The researching author's observations and interpretations, not confirmation of every disaster survivor's feelings. Applying this to ordinary partings is the kit's proposal; do not equate them with disaster experience.

## S17 Areas of trust and the amount of secrecy

- Source (original Japanese record): [荒川弘氏へのインタビュー](https://kyoko-np.net/2025040101.html)。発信：虚構新聞。公開：2025-04-01。通常の風刺記事と異なり、記事冒頭に実インタビューである旨の表示があることを確認。

- Limits: Creative views and personal attitudes, not a rule to reveal all secrets or a psychological law of relationships. Illustrations were not viewed here.

## S18 Inner life not determined by expressions, and communication

- Source (original Japanese record): [大今良時氏インタビュー・後半](https://www.acc-cm.or.jp/publications/acction/adventure/2016.06/page2.html)。発信：ACC、取材：丸山顕。公開日は取得本文では確認できず。
- Confirmed claim: Explains the intent to avoid directly showing inner thoughts, preserve the possibility that expressions and feelings differ, and communicate beyond words.
- Application: W01–W05, Q01, Q02, Q04, and Q05, separating observation from inferred thoughts and checking relationship change through behavior.
- Limits: The article does not formulate a reconciliation process. Distinguishing apology, forgiveness, and resumed relationships is the kit's proposal. It does not justify reducing bullying or harm to simple mutual misunderstanding.

## Scope of illustration inspection

On 2026-09-19, three illustrations were actually opened: S01's arrangement of protagonist and surrounding characters, S04's comparison of a composed expression with a blushing response, and S06's cover character. Only the published composition, expressions, and gestures were confirmed, not full works or general reader emotions. In particular, S04 illustrates contrasting reactions, not verified allure.

Do not include reproduced illustrations or article text in the distribution. Identify sources and express necessary points in original wording. This kit's license does not apply to third-party material.

On the same date, for the next four themes, one illustration in S12 was opened showing sound through trees, a person, and dense surrounding linework. Textual elements indicating the sound event were also visible; meaning was not judged to arise from abstract lines alone. Separate authorial intent, visible arrangement, and hypotheses about reader effects. Keep images only in research records excluded from distribution.

On the same date, for the remaining nine themes, two illustrations were opened: S16's combination of inner thoughts, flashback, and a person looking at the sky; and S23's comparison of fine everyday actions with large-action panels. The first is not evidence of an established romance. The second is an article-edited illustration, not direct confirmation of the original publication's page-turn experience. Claims are limited to comparing arrangement and text against the author's explanation.

## Current biases and unverified scope

- Main straight-man response sources lean toward Shueisha lectures and author interviews. Comparison with editorial judgments from other publishers remains future work.
- Direct allure sources focus on single illustrations. Applications to comics are production proposals using shared knowledge of performance and viewpoint.
- Complete books and serials, applicability across broad styles and audiences, and comparative trial reads with actual readers remain unverified.
- The next four themes likewise mainly combine creator views with the kit's adaptations. Coolness leans toward shonen manga; anger toward character beliefs and romance; loss toward bereavement and disasters. Application to work outside competition, nonromantic arguments, and losses other than bereavement remains unverified.
- The remaining nine themes also mainly adapt creator views and common knowledge. Affection and endearment lean toward romance, urgency toward mystery, and bargaining toward works about games. Stages of reconciliation are this kit's proposal; do not quote them as a creator's prescribed process.
- The 15 added themes are experimental. Distinguish preliminary textual review from checking image quality and emotional effects. Do not generalize an individual trial-read impression into evidence across all themes and readers.
