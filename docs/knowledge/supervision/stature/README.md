# Supervision: Coolness and Stature

Status: Experimental. Updated / sources checked: 2026-09-19. Use to convey skill, judgment, resolve, and presence in a scene. Also applies to work, competition, and daily life outside combat. These are **production proposals** informed by creators' approaches and common knowledge, not demonstrated reader effects.

Main sources: [S09](../sources.md#s09) and [S10](../sources.md#s10). Compare Hiro Mashima's views on strength with Satoshi Mizukami's views on everyday life, supporting characters, and winning and losing. For choices under pressure and differences in ability, see [Common character knowledge](../../manga/05-story-character.md).

<a id="k01"></a>

## K01 Decide what kind of coolness to show

- Examine: Is the focus skill, judgment, conviction, accepted responsibility, or visual presence? Separate a character's admiration from a showcase for readers.
- Use when: “Make them look stronger” does not identify a revision.
- Compare: Demonstrate skill through results, show a difficult judgment, or showcase an entrance. Narrow the present focus even for the same person.
- Costs and exceptions: Proving ability at every visual showcase stalls the flow. Intimidating strength and personal likability need not coincide.
- Basis: Organizing dimensions of appeal with reference to S09 and S10.

<a id="k02"></a>

## K02 Provide a comparison and the reason for the difference

- Examine: Are the difficulty of the task or opponent, conditions for success, and the character's intervention clear? Is praise substituting for ability? Separate who spreads a reputation from what the story actually establishes.
- Use when: Victory or resolution is shown, but the particular excellence is unreadable.
- Compare: Establish one difficulty first, preserve an effective move by the opponent, or show different judgments under the same constraint.
- Costs and exceptions: More explanation may reduce speed or overwhelming impact. Do not suddenly make the opponent incompetent or erase established ability limits. Rumors and others' reactions can foreshadow stature in an introduction, but unproven reputation differs from confirmed ability.
- Basis: S09's depiction of opponents and S10's treatment of supporting characters and outcomes.

<a id="k03"></a>

## K03 Show what was chosen and what was accepted

- Examine: What did the character protect, give up, and choose to do? Does the signature line connect to an actual decision?
- Use when: Resolve is proclaimed but no behavioral difference is visible.
- Compare: Accept danger, seek help to fulfill responsibility, or withdraw from a pointless contest. Compare choices suited to the scene.
- Costs and exceptions: More self-sacrifice is not a goal in itself. If lightness or composure is intended, retain an option without added anguish.
- Basis: Applying S09, S10, and common knowledge of choices under pressure to this effect.

<a id="k04"></a>

## K04 Allocate space between the action and surrounding reactions

- Examine: Are the person's act and observers' responses readable? Do surprise, renewed readiness, or entrusting something add beyond explaining ability?
- Use when: More large panels or praise fails to strengthen the impression.
- Compare: Showcase the action at scale, accompany a small gesture with changed behavior around it, or show the impact before the person arrives.
- Costs and exceptions: Universal admiration erases others' experience or rivalry. Large panels, spreads, and low angles are options, not requirements; reject them when they do not suit the format or action.
- Basis: S09's allocation of showcases and common knowledge of viewpoint and reaction order.

<a id="k05"></a>

## K05 Preserve relationships to weakness, daily life, and outcomes

- Examine: Has this showcase erased established limitations or everyday roles? Is victory being treated as proof of personal worth or correct beliefs?
- Use when: A revision makes someone all-powerful and loses their identity or future challenges.
- Compare: Delegate a weakness, show what was protected despite defeat, depict responsibility after combat, or preserve composure here.
- Costs and exceptions: Weaknesses and daily-life detail are not mandatory insertions either. A mysterious entrance or a style that celebrates perfection may defer explanation.
- Basis: Use S10's approach as an alternative, not as a required protagonist model for every work.

## Comparison, images, and trial reads

Hold the challenge, ability, and result constant. Compare only necessary differences among evidence of action / others' reactions, composure / uncertainty, or victory / what was protected. In PNGs, check starting action and result, effective opposing actions, and reading order of the showcase. Attractive appearance does not verify ability.

In trial reads, first ask what the person seemed good at and which detail suggested it, then ask about coolness and taste. Follow [Common supervision procedures](../routing.md) for item numbers, observations, hypotheses, acceptance, and costs. Comparative verification with actual project PNGs and readers has not been performed.
