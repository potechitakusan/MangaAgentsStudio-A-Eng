# Supervision: Crisis, Anxiety, and Urgency

Status: Experimental. Updated / sources checked: 2026-09-19. Covers scenes where possible failure approaches and the present choice matters. These are **production proposals** informed by [S19](../sources.md#s19), [S22](../sources.md#s22), and [S23](../sources.md#s23). Organizing them by deadlines, resources, and remaining opportunities is this kit's adaptation, not a law stated directly by the sources.

For a threat's identity or presence, see [Unease and fear](../horror/README.md); for release through resolution, [Payoff](../payoff/README.md). For causal action and time, see [Scenes](../../manga/04-scene-dialogue-props.md) and [Pacing](../../manga/03-beat-pacing-timing.md).

## U01 Specify what failure would cost

- Examine: What must be protected or accomplished, and what follows failure? Why does it matter to this person? If the ending is known, which choice, process, or cost on the way remains urgent?
- Use when: Hurried dialogue does not explain why being late matters.
- Compare: Supply one loss already in the work: a closing service counter, a broken promise, or one final opportunity.
- Costs and exceptions: Do not add death or massive damage solely for urgency. A small task can work if it matters greatly to the person.
- Basis: Extending S19's willingness to allow failure to crises including everyday life.

<a id="u02"></a>

## U02 Show changes in remaining time, means, and opportunities

- Examine: What decreases, and which option becomes unavailable? Are numbers and positions consistent across the sequence?
- Use when: A countdown changes nothing or a limit is extended afterward.
- Compare: Lose an available route, complete a step at a resource cost, or change a deadline through someone else's action.
- Costs and exceptions: A visible clock is optional. Merely tightening restrictions can remove room for ingenuity.
- Basis: S22's key points and choices, plus common causality knowledge.

<a id="u03"></a>

## U03 Create anxiety by connecting attempts to results

- Examine: What does the character try, how do they interpret the result, and what do they do next? Does anxiety make them forget established knowledge or skills?
- Use when: Shouting and running continue without visible progress.
- Compare: Let the best move partly fail, request cooperation, or narrow the goal and give something up.
- Costs and exceptions: Anxiety while unable to act is also valid. Show what prevents action and why the wait still matters.
- Basis: Applying S22's method of deriving choices from characters' own thinking.

<a id="u04"></a>

## U04 Keep clear what a waiting panel is waiting for

- Examine: The awaited reply, movement, or completed task, and its progress.
- Use when: Assuming smaller panels always read faster causes an important judgment to be skipped.
- Compare: Sequence brief actions, devote more space to an instant of decision, or contrast still waiting with progress elsewhere.
- Costs and exceptions: Subdivision can make time feel longer. Check actual print, single-page, and scrolling displays.
- Basis: S23's pacing and common knowledge, not measured reading speed.

<a id="u05"></a>

## U05 Check which conditions remain after a breather

- Examine: What is resolved and what remains dangerous? Is a small success treated as a solution to everything?
- Use when: Crisis continues too long, or one joke erases even the constraints.
- Compare: Finish one stage, secure the next means, or feel temporary relief before resuming.
- Costs and exceptions: A pause reduces tension but can make room to read the next change. Do not maintain maximum anxiety constantly.
- Basis: Adapting S19's attention to everyday brightness and how events are received.

## Comparison, images, and trial reads

Keep deadlines, resources, abilities, and outcome constant. Compare when limits appear and how finely action is divided. In PNGs, check positions, remaining quantities, and the order of results. In trial reads, ask what may be too late and which action changed the options. Comparative verification with actual project PNGs and readers has not been performed.
