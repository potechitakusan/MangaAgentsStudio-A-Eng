# Independent review of example dialogue localization

Date: 2026-09-30. Independent draft-stage semantic review, preserved for localization 0.4.1-en-US.2. The reviewer is separate from the script authors. The reviewed scripts are preserved as text in [the example editing record](example-edit-prompts.json); selected images are mapped in [the image manifest](example-localization.json). Working filenames below identify the private review inputs. This record predates final-image adoption; actual-image verification is recorded separately in [image review](REVIEW-example-images.md).

All proposed narrative lettering was read against its Japanese transcription and paraphrased back into Japanese. All 29 source pages were visually inspected: examples 01 (2), 02 (3), 03 (4), 05 (8), and 06 (12). Example 01's original PNGs could not be displayed because the image-view tool returned base64 transport errors; the existing `example-inspection/01-page-001.jpg` and `01-page-002.jpg` inspection copies were used instead. All other pages were opened from their actual source PNG/JPEG files. The visible text and character/prop context supported the supplied transcriptions. No new OCR or transcription of unreadable background marks was invented.

The scripts are semantically ready for image drafting with the five corrections below. Speaker ambiguity in two Example 06 balloons remains unresolved and must not be silently replaced with a newly invented attribution. This review covers source meaning and proposed lettering, not whether a generated English image follows the script.

## Examples 02 and 03 — announcement and pictorial communication

Example 02's announcement remains an announcement: `それでは` → “And now...” → 「それでは」; `魔法トーナメント 決勝…` → “The final round of the magic tournament...” → 「魔法トーナメントの決勝…」; banner `決勝` → “FINALS”; `始めッ!!` → “BEGIN!!”. No victor, attack name, character name, or additional command is supplied. Pages 2–3 contain action and a concealing flash without legible narrative wording to translate. Preserving the ice character on the left and fire character on the right retains their established geography; their paired actions can be read left to right without imposing a new winner.

Example 03's title `雨の日の約束` → “A Promise on a Rainy Day” → 「雨の日の約束」 is faithful. Rain, flowers, bridge, faces, hearts, punctuation symbols, and pinky promises stay pictorial. They do not become literal spoken dialogue or proof of a specifically named relationship.

Added concrete LTR sequencing notes to the private 01–03 specification after viewing all four Example 03 pages: swap the page-1 explanation/proposal row; swap page-2 notice/explanation row; keep page-3 simultaneous spellcasting in the existing left/right river geography; swap page-4 bud/reaction row. Full-width reveals retain their vertical order. These are panel-order adaptations, not image mirroring or invented events.

The three exact repetition sets were compared explicitly:

- P02-05, P05-05, P08-04: `明日には国境だ` → **We'll reach the border tomorrow.** → 「明日には国境に着く」. Keep all three identical; repeated balloons with no visible speaker tail do not receive a named speaker.

Page 8's question must precede the elf's reply in the new balloon order despite their unchanged right/left character positions. The plan to stack balloons at different reading levels preserves that exchange. The final steaming cup and repeated border line remain unexplained.

The mathematical and detective jokes are adapted by meaning. English has no identical written 迷/名 homophone, so “wild deductions” versus “master/incredible detective” preserves the contrast. Names keep the Japanese characters and school setting; given-name-first order is an editorial convention. School years remain First Year and Second Year rather than invented US grades or ages. Provisional surname romanizations remain subject to an author's established spelling.

The two attribution issues remain visible in the actual source:

1. **009-P2-B, ごめんね / Sorry.** The apology balloon's tail points toward Koharu's side, while Komugi's previous line admits carrying the tin away. Do not turn the translation into “I'm sorry I took your bookmark,” which would decide speaker and add culpability. Keep “Sorry.” and preserve the original attribution cue pending the author's decision.
2. **010-P3-B, the special seat.** Placement/tail is near Rin and the table; the relaxed ending is also consistent with Komugi's voice. Preserve its existing attribution cue rather than rerouting it to a newly chosen speaker. The revised English establishes the seat's location, not the speaker's identity.

Off-panel voices marked probable in the transcript remain probable. No narrator label, character name, or dialogue tag is added to force attribution.
