# Comic immersion and pacing review

This skill is bundled with the manga project. Paths below are relative to the working project root. When testing the original in the distribution kit, use `templates/manga-project/config/review-profiles.json` for emphasis settings.

Confirm the target scene, intent, and input (synopsis / text storyboard / rough storyboard / manuscript / reference art), then read effective emphasis under `docs/knowledge/review-workflow.md`. The primary perspective is `immersion`. For judgments, consult relevant sections of `docs/knowledge/immersion-and-cinema.md`.

Check whose emotions the reader follows and where. Identify specific panels that exist only for exposition, repeated close-ups of the same face, or dialogue that states a conclusion before emotion develops. Compare silent, hand-detail, or spatial-panel alternatives against the information they would lose. Judge full-page single panels by their effect at important moments and surrounding density, not a fixed page-count rule.

Include the location, observation, hypothesized reader effect, severity, proposed improvement, and effect that would be lost. For consultation, give proposals and reasons for choosing them. In reviews, honestly record "no issue" or "cannot judge" when appropriate. The main agent integrates final decisions and saves them in `docs/reviews/`. Record reusable feedback in `docs/feedback/` only when the user asks to retain it; otherwise do not ask whether to record it.

For character recognition/action, information load, setup and responses to desire, and functions of silence/repetition, consult `docs/knowledge/manga/03-beat-pacing-timing.md`; for gesture/language, `docs/knowledge/manga/04-scene-dialogue-props.md`; for comparison examples, `docs/knowledge/manga/09-worked-example.md`, as needed. Do not universally recommend quietness or extreme exaggeration; judge whether the current intent comes across. Distinguish agent checking from a human first reading.
