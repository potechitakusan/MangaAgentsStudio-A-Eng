Describe visible changes such as lowered eyes or a withdrawn hand, not merely "looks sad." Inner circumstances known only to the author are not information already conveyed to readers.

As a rule, assign one main action per panel. With multiple actions, specify the relationship readers should perceive simultaneously. For hand/tool contact, record before/during/after contact and whose hand touches what. Do not add characters to explain; split into adjacent panels if needed.

Use natural English punctuation in dialogue/narration and centered horizontal balloon lettering with word-boundary wrapping. For connected balloons/caption boxes record connections and preserve intended sentence flow; do not apply Japanese full-stop bans or character-count comma rules. See [Lettering and punctuation](../knowledge/japanese-manga-readability.md#dialogue-typesetting).

Only for flashbacks, omissions, or parallel action, annotate relevant panels with scene/time identity, event chronology versus presentation order, narration time versus depicted time, and transition/return cues. Do not add time-switch fields to panels without time changes.
